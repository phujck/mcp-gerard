# Admin and Proposal Register

**Status**: schema scaffold. Evidence pending proposal and admin corpus ingestion.

This register handles documents written under institutional constraint: proposals, applications, summaries, and administrative forms. These texts may be weak as writing while still revealing how the author fights bad form.

## What To Extract

- salvageable framing moves
- recurring places where the form flattens the thought
- vocabulary that appears under compliance pressure
- attempts to translate structural ideas for hostile or indifferent readers
- failure modes that should be actively corrected in manuscript drafting

## Gate 2 Distilled Updates from 55-Ledger Synthesis (2026-06-01)

Source handles: ADMIN-02 through ADMIN-16 (15 admin/proposal ledgers: grant proposals, cover letters, fellowship applications, a CV summary). Distilled, provenance-tagged.

**The voice survives institutional form by claiming ownership explicitly (ADMIN-02, ADMIN-03, ADMIN-05).**
When allowed first-person agency, the voice takes it without apology: "I developed this methodology", "I am not seeking to achieve incremental improvements, but to wring life from a framework I can truly call my own." The strategic move is to name who did what, then let the work's scope speak. In manuscript prose (sole-authored), the same applies: claim the construction, then let the construction carry the argument.

**Constraint-as-feature across all proposal registers (ADMIN-02 through ADMIN-13).**
The voice consistently reframes practical limitations as generative features: dissipation becomes a resource; the messiness of real systems becomes the motivation for the method; the absence of a quantum vocabulary becomes the exact problem the work solves. This is the grant-proposal manifestation of the general voice mechanic. In manuscript prose, the failure condition of a prior method is the entry point to the new construction, not a problem to apologise for.

**Bluntness as credibility signal (ADMIN-02, ADMIN-05).**
When the voice admits a practical impossibility directly - "This is - bluntly - impossible, so we must make do with a synecdoche" - the admission earns the workaround. The bluntness is a rhetorical investment that pays dividends on the pragmatic pivot that follows. In manuscript prose, the equivalent is the explicit statement of what the method cannot do, placed before the statement of what it can. The honesty makes the capability credible.

**Proof-before-extrapolation is consistent across all proposals (ADMIN-02 through ADMIN-13).**
Every proposal ledger confirms this pattern: named prior result or hardware demonstration, then the proposed extension, then the reason the extension follows naturally from the prior result. No proposal claims capability without grounding it in evidence that already exists. The pattern "proof-of-principle calculations have already been implemented in hardware" is a structurally mandatory sentence in this register.

**Narrative coherence as a form of persuasion (ADMIN-03, ADMIN-04).**
The strongest proposals use narrative resonance rather than enumeration as their primary persuasion mode. The concept of "emulation" (one form of dynamics wearing the mask of another) appears in both the technical content and the personal disclosure of the fellowship application - form enacts content. In manuscript prose, the equivalent is: the metaphor you use for the framing should be the same metaphor you use for the mechanism. Do not switch metaphors between the introduction and the conclusion.

**Technical vocabulary as a proxy for ownership (ADMIN-08, ADMIN-09, ADMIN-10).**
In cover letters and institutional applications, the voice resists explicit self-promotion and lets the technical vocabulary carry the authority signal: "I introduced a novel approach for mapping non-unitary dynamics to constructive averages of unitary operations, dubbed quantum dynamical emulation." The passive-adjacent framing ("dubbed") distances the voice from the naming while the precise technical language demonstrates the depth of knowledge. In manuscript prose, this translates to: let the formalism demonstrate the understanding; do not announce that the formalism demonstrates the understanding.

**Collaboration as credential, not support (ADMIN-09, ADMIN-10, ADMIN-12).**
In institutional documents, the voice names collaborators and institutions not as social proof but as precision signals: Sandia National Laboratory and its superconducting qubit processor is evidence of hardware access, not name-dropping. Each collaborator reference serves a specific technical positioning function. In manuscript prose, citations serve the same purpose: cite to position the work in a specific technical context, not to signal engagement with the field.

**Identifying the register's failure modes.**
Admin documents are where the voice is most likely to produce the anti-patterns identified in the kill-list: "second quantum revolution", "rich tapestry of opportunities", "transformative potential", "opening new avenues." These appear in the proposals and must be actively removed. The rule: any sentence that could appear unchanged in any grant proposal in any subfield of physics is a failure-mode sentence. Name the specific object. State the specific consequence.

## Evidence Quality

Admin and proposal documents usually begin as mixed or failure-mode evidence. A reader may promote a passage only when the passage clearly carries a durable voice move despite the form.

Gate 2 update: After reading 15 proposal ledgers, the evidence quality is higher than the schema scaffold assumed. The proposals contain clear voice mechanics - they are not primarily failure-mode documents. Promote the framing moves listed above to confirmed evidence. The failure modes are also genuinely present and should be flagged at the anti-pattern stage of any redraft.

## Manuscript Transfer

This register is most useful as a warning system. It identifies what happens when the voice is forced to justify itself in a frame that does not understand the work.

Gate 2 update: The register also provides positive transfer rules. In particular: bluntness as credibility signal, proof-before-extrapolation as mandatory structure, and narrative coherence as persuasion mode all transfer directly to manuscript introductions and discussion sections.
